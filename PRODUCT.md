# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Small and mid-size business owners and their operations staff. They deploy three AI employees (Reception, Sales, Marketing) that answer customers on WhatsApp, Instagram, web chat, and email. Users check the dashboard between other work: they need to see at a glance whether the agents are performing, jump into escalated conversations, and keep the knowledge base current. Not technical; they trust interfaces that resemble Notion, Linear, and modern banking dashboards.

## Product Purpose

Harbor AI is an agentic business operating system: multi-tenant SaaS where each workspace runs autonomous AI agents that converse with customers, qualify leads, book appointments, and sync to CRMs. The dashboard is the control room: agent performance, conversations, leads, knowledge, channels, billing. Success = the owner trusts the agents enough to leave them running, and the dashboard proves that trust with real numbers.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, premium. "A $100M SaaS" feel: quiet confidence, generous whitespace, one electric-blue accent, ink-black feature moments. Never playful-cartoonish, never enterprise-grey.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-purple gradient slop; mesh-gradient heroes.
- Bootstrap-admin density: cramped tables, grey-on-grey chrome.
- Dumb-chatbot aesthetics (bubbly mascots, emoji-heavy copy).
- Fingoals/Dribbble shots copied literally: we borrow the spatial grammar (card rhythm, dark feature cards, pill navigation), not the content.

## Design Principles

1. **The numbers are the hero.** Metrics readable in one glance; chrome recedes.
2. **One interactive color.** Electric blue (#2563EB) marks everything clickable; nothing else competes.
3. **Dark cards are moments, not themes.** Ink-black (#0f0f0f) reserved for quick actions and the upgrade banner, in both light and dark modes.
4. **Real states everywhere.** Skeletons that match layout, empty states that teach, errors that offer retry.
5. **Same vocabulary on every screen.** One card style (16px radius, thin border, 2-layer shadow), one switch, one chip, one button family.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Status never color-only (always text label). Focus-visible rings on all interactive elements. Skip link in app shell. Reduced-motion honored for all animation. Dark mode follows system preference.
