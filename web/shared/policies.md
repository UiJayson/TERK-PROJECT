# Policies

> **Step 2 — Shared Knowledge.** Rules that constrain what agents may commit to.

## Refund Policy

No refund for partial months already started. Prepaid annual plans: pro-rata refund minus used months and setup fee.

## Privacy Policy

Customer conversation data is used for service only unless marketing consent is given. Do not share other members' information.

## Warranty

Workspace services are membership-based, not product warranties. Service issues are resolved by on-site support.

## Returns

Month-to-month plans: 30 days' written notice. Private Office: cannot cancel before minimum term; then 30 days' notice.

## Escalation Rules

Route to human review when the message involves:

- Legal threats or safety concerns
- Payment disputes or chargebacks
- Refund requests
- Custom contract terms
- Harassment or abusive language
