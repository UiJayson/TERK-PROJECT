# FAQ

> **Step 2 — Shared Knowledge.** Short approved answers for common questions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is Harbor Workspace?**  
A: Flexible workspace — hot desks, dedicated desks, private offices, and meeting rooms in Manchester.

**Q: Can I visit before signing up?**  
A: Yes. Book a free tour through Reception — weekdays and Saturday slots are available.

**Q: What are your opening hours?**  
A: Monday–Friday 8:00 AM–8:00 PM, Saturday 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Closed Sunday.

**Q: Where are you located?**  
A: 12 Riverside Lane, Manchester, M1 4AB. We serve Greater Manchester and remote members on the Virtual Desk plan.

**Q: How do I book a tour?**  
A: Message Reception with your preferred day and contact details. We confirm by email or phone.

**Q: What is a Hot Desk?**  
A: Flexible shared desk access with Wi-Fi, kitchen access, and 4 meeting-room hours per month. No long-term contract.

**Q: What is a Dedicated Desk?**  
A: Your own fixed desk with locker, mail handling, and 8 meeting-room hours per month.

**Q: What is a Private Office?**  
A: A lockable furnished office for teams of 2–12, with 20 meeting-room hours per month and branded door sign.

**Q: What is the Virtual Desk plan?**  
A: Business address and mail handling without a physical desk — ideal for remote freelancers and SMEs.

**Q: How much does a Hot Desk cost?**  
A: £199/month excluding VAT. Two months free when paid annually.

**Q: How much does a Dedicated Desk cost?**  
A: £349/month excluding VAT. Two months free when paid annually.

**Q: How much does a Private Office cost?**  
A: From £1,200/month (4-person) or £1,650/month (6-person), excluding VAT. Minimum 3-month term.

**Q: Is Wi-Fi included?**  
A: Yes. Reliable business-grade Wi-Fi is included in all desk and office plans.

**Q: Can I use meeting rooms?**  
A: Yes. Hours are included per plan; extra hours can be booked at reception rates.

**Q: What payment methods do you accept?**  
A: Credit/debit card, bank transfer, and direct debit. VAT is added at checkout.

**Q: Can I cancel my membership?**  
A: Month-to-month plans require 30 days' written notice. Private Office plans have a minimum term — see policies.md.

**Q: Do you offer refunds?**  
A: No refund for partial months already started. Prepaid annual plans: pro-rata refund minus used months and setup fee.

**Q: Is parking available?**  
A: Limited on-site spaces; nearby pay-and-display and multi-storey car parks within 5 minutes' walk.

**Q: Can I bring guests?**  
A: Yes, with a day pass or as part of a booked meeting room. Reception can arrange guest access.

**Q: How do I get support?**  
A: Email hello@harborworkspace.example or call +44 161 555 0100. Reception handles first contact; complex issues escalate to a manager.

## Shipping

We are a local workspace — there is no product shipping. Virtual Desk mail handling is collected on-site or forwarded per your plan.

## Returns

Membership cancellations follow our refund policy in policies.md. No returns apply to workspace services.

## Delivery

Membership access begins on your start date. Meeting rooms and tours are booked by appointment.

## Payments

We accept card, bank transfer, and direct debit. VAT is added at checkout. Invoices are sent monthly or annually.
