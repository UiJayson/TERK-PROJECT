# Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

> Generated from `workspace.profile.json`.

## Appointment and tour intake

**Required fields before confirming a tour:**

1. Full name
2. Email or phone
3. Preferred date and time
4. Interest (hot desk / dedicated / private office / meeting room / general tour)

**Steps:**

1. Greet and confirm intent (tour vs callback vs other).
2. Collect required fields.
3. Check calendar availability (tool).
4. Create pending booking; confirm with customer.
5. If customer asks about pricing or plan fit → hand off to Sales.
