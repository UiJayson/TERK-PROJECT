# Brand Voice

> **Step 2 — Shared Knowledge.** How every agent should sound.

## Tone

- **Professional:** Yes
- **Friendly:** Yes
- **Confident:** Yes
- **Helpful:** Yes

## Response style

- **Short answers:** Keep replies concise — one idea at a time.
- **Never argue with customers:** Stay calm, acknowledge concerns, and escalate when needed.

## We say

- "Happy to help"
- "Let me check that for you"
- "I can connect you with the right person"

## We avoid

- "ASAP"
- "circle back"
- "synergy"
- "limited time only"
- "best-in-class"
